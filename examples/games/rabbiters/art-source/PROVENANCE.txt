Rabbiters original procedural cast, revision 1.
All six concept-v1 SVG illustrations were authored from geometric primitives for this task, then motion-v1 SVG sheets were derived before scene.ts modeling. No downloads, external models, textures, generative services, or third-party character art.
For rabbit, fox, wolf, hawk, snake, crocodile: exact source paths are <kind>-concept-v1.svg and <kind>-motion-v1.svg in this directory. Model revision: scene.ts character() v1; motions: animate() v1. Preserve these v1 sources unchanged; subsequent revisions must use new filenames.
Sheet cells: idle, anticipation, release, contact, recoil, recovery, left to right. Models translate their rounded silhouettes, colors, ears, wings and tails into original Three.js primitives, facing local +Z. Rabbit walk/jump uses body lift and ear lag; quadrupeds use alternating stride and tail sway; hawk uses hinged wings; snake uses a connected undulating chain; crocodile uses a low stride and tail sway.
No attack timing or damage is inferred by the renderer. Simulation positions remain authoritative. Visual acceptance remains open until sprite sheets and actual in-game playback receive five documented visual review passes, including eight directions, desktop and mobile.
