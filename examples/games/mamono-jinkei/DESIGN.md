# マモノ陣形 — design

An original top-down co-op action game for up to four players. The heroes are four little monsters who take a new
body each stage (slime → rat → crow → wolf) and fight the human adventurers' guild. The core of the game is the
**formation (陣形)**: heroes standing together hit harder, take less damage, can move heavy things, and turn their
charged attack into a **co-op technique (協力技)**. Some puzzles can only be solved by **splitting up**.

This file is the contract every module is written against. Code comments are English; all player-facing text is
Japanese.

## 1. Platform

- Directory `examples/games/mamono-jinkei/`. Vite config `vite.config.mamono-jinkei.mjs` (dev port 5197), also an
  input of the site build (`vite.config.mjs`). TypeScript, no framework, Canvas 2D for the world, DOM for HUD/menus.
- Networking reuses `CooperativeNetwork` from `examples/games/rabbiters/src/network.ts` with
  `app: 'mamono-jinkei-v1'` (host-authoritative world, JSON messages ≤ 64 KiB). Max 4 heroes; extra guests spectate.
- Art is generated by code at runtime into indexed rasters (`src/art/`), no image files. Music and sound effects
  are synthesized with Web Audio from scores in code (`src/audio/`), no audio files.
- Desktop first: keyboard + mouse. Gamepad optional. Touch: the title explains that a keyboard and mouse are
  needed; menus still work by tap.

## 2. Units and coordinates

- World unit = one art pixel. `TILE = 64`. Character cells are 64×64 art px; bosses 128×128.
- Positions are the **feet** of a character (`x`, `y`). A 64×64 character frame is drawn with its top-left at
  `(x - 32, y - 56)`; feet sit on row 56 of the frame. A 128×128 boss frame is drawn at `(x - 64, y - 116)`.
- Hero collision: circle radius 16 at the feet. Enemies: radius by kind (12–20). Bosses: 36–44.
- Simulation time in seconds; the host steps the world at a fixed 60 Hz (`DT = 1 / 60`).
- Directions for art: `down`, `up`, `side` (side faces right; the renderer mirrors it for left).
  `dirOf(angle)` picks side when |cos| ≥ 0.6, else down/up.

## 3. Camera and rendering

- Base view height is **576 art px** (9 tiles); width follows the window aspect (16:9 → 1024). Boss arenas zoom
  out to 720 px tall. Each client's camera follows its own controlled hero with smoothing and a small lead toward
  the mouse, clamped to the map.
- Pixel-perfect "sharp bilinear" scaling: the world is drawn at 1 art px per pixel into an offscreen canvas,
  upscaled by the integer `ceil(scale)` with smoothing off, then drawn to the screen canvas with smoothing on.
  Sprite positions are rounded to whole art pixels.
- Draw order: ground chunks (pre-rendered 16×16-tile chunks) → floor decals (plates, cracks, shadows, formation
  rings) → y-sorted actors and tall props → projectiles → overhead (canopies, arches) → particles and damage
  numbers → lighting/weather overlay → screen effects (vignette, flash, iris) → DOM HUD.
- Juice: hit-stop (50–90 ms) on heavy hits, screen shake (reduced with `prefers-reduced-motion`), white hit flash,
  knockback, squash and stretch, dust on turns and landings, damage numbers, item magnet and bounce.

## 4. Party, jobs and slots

The party is always the four jobs. Slot = job, so the job is the hero's identity and colour.

| slot | job id   | 名前     | colour | HP  | normal attack (click)                                          | cooldown | dmg |
| ---- | -------- | -------- | ------ | --- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | --- |
| 0    | `sword`  | 剣士     | red    | 10  | slash arc, radius 58, 120° toward the aim                      | 0.30 s   | 2   |
| 1    | `mage`   | 魔法使い | blue   | 8   | magic bolt 380 px/s, range 7 tiles, bursts radius 30 on hit    | 0.45 s   | 2   |
| 2    | `archer` | 弓使い   | green  | 8   | arrow 680 px/s, range 9 tiles, pierces 1 enemy                 | 0.34 s   | 2   |
| 3    | `cleric` | 僧侶     | gold   | 9   | staff bonk radius 46, 100°; every 4th hit heals formation 1 HP | 0.38 s   | 1   |

HP is in half-hearts (10 = 5 hearts). Charged attack when **not** in formation (job skill):

- 剣士 `回転斬り`: 360° radius 84, dmg 4, strong knockback.
- 魔法使い `大魔弾`: orb 260 px/s, bursts radius 92, dmg 5.
- 弓使い `三連矢`: three arrows 18° apart, dmg 3 each, pierce 2.
- 僧侶 `聖なる輪`: ring radius 100: heals allies inside 3 HP, dmg 3 to enemies.

Each human owns one or more heroes. Slots not taken by a human are **companions** owned by a human (assigned
round-robin from the host). With one human the player owns all four (like a squad). A human controls one owned
hero at a time; `Tab` cycles to the next owned hero (the previous one becomes a companion). Companions are
simulated on the host.

Companion modes (`Q` toggles for all your companions):

- **follow (ついてこい)**: move to formation spots around the owner (behind-left, behind-right, behind; keep 56 px
  apart), path-find on the tile grid, use species traversal (jump gaps, dash holes, fly pits) when the path needs
  it. Mimic the owner: attack in the owner's aim direction (staggered 0.04 s each), charge while the owner charges,
  grip heavy objects the owner grips, push with the owner. Fight back against enemies within 2 tiles when the owner
  is idle. Catch up with a puff if > 12 tiles away and the owner's tile is reachable.
- **stay (待て)**: hold position (stand on plates), face the owner's last aim, defend against enemies within 1.5
  tiles, and on the **せーの GO** signal perform one normal attack in their facing direction (rings bells, hits
  crystals) — this lets one player solve simultaneous puzzles.

While charging, move at 55% speed. Charge levels: 0–0.9 s building, then **FULL** (flash + ping). Releasing before
full does a normal attack.

## 6. Formation (陣形)

- Two alive, non-downed heroes are **linked** when their distance ≤ 104 px. A connected component with ≥ 2 heroes
  whose members are all within 150 px of its centroid is a **formation** of size n (2 = ペア陣, 3 = 三角陣,
  4 = 四方陣). Recomputed every tick; a hero is in at most one formation.
- Effects for members: normal attack damage × (1 + 0.3·(n−1)); damage taken × (1 − 0.1·(n−1)); push/carry strength
  n; full-charge release → co-op technique of tier n. Cleric heal-on-hit applies to formation members.
- Visuals: glowing links between linked members, a ring at the centroid with the label (「三角陣」) and the members'
  job icons; the ring pulses when any member is fully charged; a banner when a formation of 4 forms the first time
  in an area.
- After a co-op technique every member has a 2 s charge lockout.

## 7. Species (one per stage)

| stage | species | speed   | Space action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----- | ------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | `slime` | 150     | **ぷるんジャンプ**: 0.5 s hop (36 px high), +20% speed, clears 1-tile gaps (`gap` tiles, water channels, spikes). Landing shockwave r40: dmg 1 + knockback. Bounce pads launch a 3-tile jump.                                                    |
| 2     | `rat`   | 190     | **チョロ走り**: 0.22 s dash at 3× speed, invulnerable, cooldown 0.6 s. Dashing into a **mouse hole** passes through to its paired hole.                                                                                                          |
| 3     | `crow`  | 165/205 | **羽ばたき**: fly while held, 1.5 s stamina (regains 1 s/s on the ground). Flying crosses pits, water and low fences; wind currents push flyers. Landing on a pit = fall.                                                                        |
| 4     | `wolf`  | 180→240 | **遠吠え**: 0.5 s howl, cooldown 2.5 s: stuns enemies within 120 px for 0.8 s and lights moonstones within 160 px for 4 s. Two or more howls within 0.6 s near a big moonstone = 共鳴. Wolves sprint (×1.35) after 0.6 s of continuous movement. |

Co-op techniques (tier n = formation size, 2..4; members are invulnerable while it runs):

1. **ジャンボ・プレス** (slime): members slide to the centroid (0.25 s) and merge into one big jumbo slime (ジャンボスライム, original design: marbled job colours, no crown)
   (native sprites at 3 sizes), which leaps to the aim point (≤ 4 tiles) and slams: radius 90/120/150,
   dmg 6/9/13, stun 1 s. Breaks `cracked` floor/boulders, presses `bigplate`s. Members pop back out radially.
2. **チューチュー大行進** (rat): members line up behind the releaser and stampede toward the aim for
   6/8/10 tiles over 0.7/0.9/1.1 s, width 48/56/64, dmg 4/6/9 to each enemy on the path, gnaws through
   `barricade`s. Members end at the endpoint (stopping at walls).
3. **黒羽の大旋風** (crow): members circle the aim point (≤ 5 tiles) as a tornado for 1.6/2.0/2.6 s, radius
   80/100/130, pulls enemies in, 1 dmg every 0.2 s, spins `windmill`s, blows away `fog` and `dust` piles, pulls
   flying bosses down.
4. **月下群狼陣** (wolf): a joint howl stuns every enemy within 5 tiles for 1.2 s, then each member lunges at up
   to n distinct enemies in range (dmg 5/6/8 per lunge), finishing with a crescent wave toward the aim (dmg 4/6/8).
   Lights every moonstone within 5 tiles (big moonstones need tier ≥ 3) and shatters `icewall`s in range.

Job contributions to a technique when that job is in the formation: 剣士 +20% damage, 魔法使い an extra burst at
the end (r70, dmg 3), 弓使い an arrow rain on up to 4 enemies (dmg 2), 僧侶 heals members 2 HP afterwards.

## 8. Heavy objects

- **Push (grid)**: pushable blocks sit on the tile grid. A hero pushes block B toward d when touching the face
  opposite d, input·d ≥ 0.7, on the ground and not carrying. Strength = pushing heroes + their formation mates
  whose input·d ≥ 0.7 (cap 4). With strength ≥ weight for 0.3 s the block slides one tile over 0.4 s if the target
  tile is free floor; into a `pit`/`water` tile it sinks and fills it. Weight 1 (crate), 2 (stone block), 4 (boulder).
  Ice blocks keep sliding until blocked. Not enough strength: strain animation, sweat, and a meter 「おもい！ 2/4」
  above the block with filled/empty paw pips.
- **Lift (small, weight 1)**: pots, barrels, jars. `E` lifts when facing within 40 px; carried overhead; `E` puts it
  down; click throws it 3 tiles (breaks on impact, 2 dmg, drops items).
- **Grip (heavy, weight 2–4)**: big objects (giant acorn, cheese wheel, golden bell, log sled). `E` near it grips
  (hero locks next to it with an effort pose). Every gripping hero adds 1; formation companions in follow mode grip
  with their owner. When grips ≥ weight it lifts: carriers move at 60% speed, the object floats above their
  centroid. A carrier who presses `E` or strays > 120 px releases; below weight it drops (thud, small shake).
  Dropped on its **pedestal** it triggers that pedestal.

## 9. Puzzle parts and conditions

- `plate` (any hero, block or heavy object), `plate2` (two heroes or a block), `bigplate` (only a co-op technique
  landing or a weight-4 object), `crystal` (toggles when hit; `timed` crystals stay on for N s), `bell` (hit to
  ring; a bell group is satisfied when all its bells ring within 1.2 s), `lever` (`E`, latches), `pedestal`
  (accepts its object), `moonstone` (lit by howl), `windmill` (spun by tornado), `torch`.
- Gates (`gate`, `bars`, `bossdoor`) open while their condition holds, or forever after once if `latch: true`.
  Conditions: `{ all: [...] }`, `{ any: [...] }`, `{ on: partId }`, `{ bells: groupId }`, `{ clear: regionId }`,
  `{ flag: name }`.
- **Split puzzles** are built so that one formation cannot solve them: plates far apart that must be held at once,
  bells/crystals in separate alcoves that must be hit within the window (use せーの), mouse holes into separate
  rooms, dark clearings each needing its own howl.

## 10. Enemies (fantasy jobs of the adventurers' guild)

Behaviour archetypes: `melee` (approach → 0.4 s flashing windup → lunge strike → 0.6 s recover), `ranged` (keep
4–6 tiles, 0.5 s aim line telegraph, shoot), `caster` (1.0 s ground circle telegraph at a hero, then AoE),
`healer` (stays behind allies, heals the most hurt one every 3 s, flees), `shield` (blocks damage from the front
120°, clang + spark; flank or use a technique; shield bash), `dasher` (fast hit-and-run), `thrower` (lobbed bomb with
a ground marker, explodes after 0.8 s), `trapper` (places snares: immobilize 1.5 s unless an ally hits the snare),
`summoner` (sends a swooping familiar). Every enemy shows `!` when it notices a hero.

## 11. Damage, downs and checkpoints

- Contact/attack damage 1–3 half-hearts; heroes get 0.8 s invulnerability with flicker after a hit, knockback 160.
- At 0 HP a hero is **down** (spirit bubble). An ally standing within 40 px for 1.5 s revives them with 4 HP; a
  downed hero also revives alone after 12 s with 2 HP if anyone is still up. If every hero is down: 「全滅」→ the
  party restarts from the last checkpoint with full HP after 2 s; puzzles that were latched stay solved.
- Falling into a pit or deep water: 0.6 s fall, respawn at the last safe tile, −2.
- Pickups: heart (+2), big heart (full), gem (score; enemies and pots drop them), key (opens a `lock` door; keys are
  shared by the party).

## 12. Stages and flow

- Title (attract demo behind the logo) → ひとりで遊ぶ / ルームを作る / ルームに参加 → lobby (choose job, host
  chooses the stage among unlocked ones, 出発) → stage intro card → play → boss → orb → results (time, gems,
  techniques, KOs, rank S/A/B/C, MVP badges) → next stage. After stage 4: ending and staff roll.
- Stage map: ASCII rows plus a legend, explicit entity list, regions (arena locks, checkpoints, boss room,
  dialogue triggers). Stage size about 64–80 × 48–64 tiles, 8–12 minutes for a full party.
- Progress is stored in `localStorage['mamono-jinkei-v1']` (`cleared`, `best`, `settings`). The host's progress
  decides which stages can be chosen.

Stage outlines:

1. **ぷるぷる草原と古城跡** (slime): meadow tutorial (jump a water channel, cut grass, pots) → stone block
   (weight 2) and boulder (weight 4) → arena → twin plates in a hedge maze (two must stand, the others pull the
   lever behind the gate) → giant acorn (grip weight 3) onto the ruins pedestal → cracked rock (ジャンボ・プレス)
   → four crystals in four alcoves (せーの) → boss ガルド.
2. **地下水路と穀物庫** (rat, light `dim`): stone sewers lit by lamps, rising into a wooden granary.
   - **Currents** (`current` tiles, a direction per channel) push walking heroes 120 px/s downstream; a formation
     of 3+ walks against them at full speed (「陣形なら流されない」). `sludge` is deep water (fall).
   - **Mouse holes** (`hole` wall tiles in pairs): dashing into one comes out of its partner; nothing carried fits.
     Holes lead into small side rooms — split puzzles: e.g. two **valves** (`valve`, hold E 1 s) in two hole-rooms
     must be turned within 1.5 s of each other to open the **sluice** that stops a current; a lever room behind a
     hole opens a gate for the others.
   - **Crates** (`crate` weight 1, `bigcrate` 2×2 weight 4) and liftable **barrels**; **mousetraps** snap shut
     (1 dmg, snared 1.5 s) when stepped on unless a pot/barrel is thrown on them first.
   - **Cheese wheel** (`cheese`, grip weight 3) carried across a current (carriers in formation resist it) onto the
     granary **scale** (`scale`) → the granary door opens.
   - **Barricades** (`barricade`, 2×1): only the stampede (チューチュー大行進) gnaws through; one hides a shortcut.
   - Enemies: 盗賊 (dashes in, steals a gem on hit, flees), 錬金術師 (lobbed potion bombs with ground markers),
     ネズミ捕り (sets snares; a snared hero is freed when an ally hits the snare), 槍衛兵 (long-reach thrust, guard).
   - Boss **猫使いの魔女ミルダ** + her **giant cat**: the cat guards the witch (she is untouchable while the cat is
     awake) with pounces and swipes; a stampede into the cat stuns it (5 s) and knocks the witch off her broom
     (broken: vulnerable). She casts homing hex bolts and summons thieves; phase 2: the cat pounces twice.
3. **風鳴りの崖と時計塔** (crow, light `day`, windy): cliff platforms over a sea of clouds, up to a clocktower.
   - **Sky** gaps (`sky` tiles): fly over (1.5 s stamina, ~5 tiles); landing on sky = fall. **Vents** (`vent`)
     refill stamina and lift you — chains of vents cross long gaps.
   - **Wind zones** (regions with a direction and strength): flying crows are pushed hard, walking ones gently; a
     formation of 3+ resists. Wind streak particles show direction.
   - **Windmills** (`windmill`): spun by the tornado (黒羽の大旋風) → lowers **drawbridge** pieces over the sky
     (latched). **Bells** (`bell`) in four towers: all rung within 1.2 s opens the clocktower (split + せーの).
   - Crows pick up **keys** and carry them over gaps to **lock doors** (`lockdoor`); the heavy **gold bell**
     (`goldbell`, grip weight 4) must be carried to its **hook** (`hook`) on the belfry.
   - Enemies: 鷹匠 (sends a hawk minion that swoops), 弩兵 (long aim line, piercing bolt), 風の魔導士 (gusts that
     push heroes toward edges), 鐘番の修道士 (heals allies, rings a bell that shields them).
   - Boss **竜騎士ヴァルク** on his wyvern: flies out of reach (only arrows/bolts hit, ×0.5), dives along a line
     telegraph, sweeps fire breath; the tornado pulls him down (downed 5 s: full damage). Phase 2: double dives.
4. **月影の森と白銀の砦** (wolf, light `night`): a moonlit snowy forest, then the guild's silver fortress.
   - **Darkness**: each hero lights ~110 px; howls flash a big light for a moment; braziers and lit moonstones
     light their surroundings. Enemies in the dark are drawn only near light.
   - **Moonstones** (`moonstone`): a howl within 160 px lights one for 6 s; a gate opens while all stones of a set
     are lit (split: dark clearings each need their own wolf). **Big moonstones** (`bigmoon`) need 共鳴 (two
     howls within 0.6 s) or the pack technique (tier ≥ 3).
   - **Ice** floor slides; **ice blocks** (`iceblock`, weight 2) slide until blocked (sliding puzzles);
     **ice walls** (`icewall`) only shatter under the pack technique (月下群狼陣).
   - **Battering log** (`log`, grip weight 4) carried to the fortress gate's **ram slot** (`ramslot`) breaks it.
     **Snow piles** (`snowpile`) are dug with attacks (gems, keys, hearts).
   - Enemies: 狩人 (snares + arrows), 聖騎士 (shield + heals allies), 氷の魔女 (ice spikes erupting under heroes
     after a telegraph), 重装兵 (heavy guard, slow, big hits).
   - Final boss **勇者レオン**: phase 1 sword combos, dashes and a parry stance (attacks into it are reflected);
     phase 2 (awaken): holy pillars rain on telegraphs and a holy shield bubble that only the pack technique breaks.
     Defeat → the last orb → the ending.

## 13. Network model

- The **host** runs the world (`sim/`) and broadcasts a snapshot `View` at 20 Hz. Guests send `NetInput` at 30 Hz.
- **Own-hero prediction**: every client moves its controlled hero locally with the shared movement/collision code
  and reports `{x, y, vx, vy, facing, aim, state}`; the host accepts the position if it is plausible (speed check,
  not inside walls) and resolves all combat and world interaction. Host-side displacement (knockback, technique
  choreography, falls, respawns, carried speed) reaches the owner as a `warp` or `impulse` with a sequence number.
- Attack, charge release, action and interact presses are edge events with sequence numbers inside `NetInput`, so
  a dropped or merged message never loses or repeats a press.
- Everything else on a guest is interpolated between the last two snapshots (render delay ~100 ms). Visual-only
  events (hits, sounds, numbers) travel as a ring buffer of numbered events; each client plays each id once.
- Solo uses the same path without a network (the local view is produced every frame).

## 14. Audio

- Music themes: `title`, `lobby`, `meadow`, `sewer`, `tower`, `forest`, `boss`, `finalBoss`, `clear` (jingle),
  `gameover` (jingle), `ending`. A shared leitmotif (the 陣形 motif) appears in the title, each stage theme (in its
  own key and style) and the ending. Look-ahead scheduling on the AudioContext clock, master bus with compressor and
  a generated-impulse reverb, stereo placement per part, crossfades between themes.
- Sound effects are synthesized, panned by screen position, with small random pitch variation and voice limits.
