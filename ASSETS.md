# Artwork and optional packs

The public distribution includes the existing original 2D runtime roster, effects and commander artwork. The retained upstream art README identifies these as original assets created for this project, not copied Tiny Swords assets. Local production records identify built-in OpenAI image generation and offline assembly of original raster parts, including the Leaf Squire, Mage, Healer, Frost, Slime, Bat, Brute and Wraith records. Other roster records describe original generated character artwork and preserved derivatives. These are AI-generated assets, not represented as wholly human-drawn artwork.

Publication basis: the project owner's explicit publication instruction, the original-source records, and OpenAI's output ownership terms (https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/, Content section). Those terms assign output rights as between the customer and OpenAI to the extent permitted by law; they do not guarantee copyright or third-party non-infringement. Pixel Frog's Tiny Swords was a visual reference, not an included third-party asset pack. Existing upstream license notices are retained; no separate third-party Tiny Swords license is claimed for these original derivatives.

The full-roster distribution includes the approved Slime rendered frames described below and 30 optimized runtime GLBs covering all 20 unit roles. Browser acceptance covers the normal-game real-mesh path. Large source models are excluded from the public tree. The owner confirmed Meshy Pro generation and publication rights; Meshy terms distinguish paid-output ownership from the free-plan CC BY 4.0 license (https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use). Original files remain preserved locally and unchanged.

The owner approved publication of the 22 Stable Audio-generated WAV outputs in `public/tower-defense-sfx/`. These unchanged processed originals were made with Stable Audio 3 Small-SFX / optimized MLX for the non-commercial demo. The existing runtime selects 21 sounds; the retained healer attack recording is currently unselected. Model weights, encoder, inference runtime and private generation records are excluded. See the [sound asset notes](public/tower-defense-sfx/README.md) for pinned source and model-term references; no blanket commercial-use license is claimed.

The normal game uses the accepted real-mesh path for all 20 unit roles; see [the verification commands in README](README.md). It does not fall back to sprites or replacement characters when a model fails. Combat balance is unchanged. The original 2D source artwork, excluded optional packs and unadopted candidates remain preserved. Battle music is approved separately below; the approved Stable Audio SFX pack is included.

## EXTRA: viewer-only rendered frames

`public/tower-defense-viewer/sheets/slime/` contains the approved melted-base Slime 3D-rendered frame derivatives: idle, walk, attack and death in eight directions (32 clips, 1,632 frames). These PNG/JSON bytes are copied unchanged from the `slime-melt-2d-runtime-candidate.zip` delivery, SHA-256 `c2a822364a6fcc5bd9d7fad859412f2f28b7ae9d2fc08e0c15a794656f6fdd77`. Source-art ownership and Meshy Pro generation terms were reconciled before inclusion. The rendered frames are available only through the read-only EXTRA viewer; they are not substituted for real-time game models. No source `.blend`, model `.glb`, credentials or production/debug-service data are included in this viewer asset directory. Death remains non-looping at 1,200 ms.

## Approved battle music

`public/tower-defense-music/` contains three owner-supplied Suno Pro-generated recordings, approved for monster-assault BGM: two tracks chosen randomly per assault for stages 1–9, and a dedicated stage-10 boss track. The MP3s are distributed unchanged, separately from the approved Stable Audio SFX pack. See [the track provenance](public/tower-defense-music/README.md) for its checksum, generation-plan statement, and limited scope. No AI copyright or non-infringement guarantee is claimed. Battle music has its own mute and volume controls and is available with its own controls.


## Slime: real-model review

`public/tower-defense-art/models/slime/animated.glb` is the approved Slime model used by `examples/games/tower_diffence_with_jev/model-review.html`. It contains nine meshes and the authored idle, walk, attack and death clips. The selected 512 px texture/runtime-LOD derivative has SHA-256 `b5f06a8e4c86a9bf3036c9f58c5754e96465f6496290a45fabab3628c1655e7d`. Its geometry was conservatively simplified and textures resized; it is not claimed to be binary-identical to the original. Animation data and retained vertex attributes were checked against the preserved source. Private source-storage metadata was removed. This page renders actual geometry but does not by itself establish full-roster gameplay acceptance.

## Full runtime roster

`public/tower-defense-art/models/runtime-manifest.json` records every source/derivative SHA-256, byte count and optimization configuration. The selected 30 GLBs total 186,209,172 bytes. Authored material, dual-quaternion skinning and additional-weight adapters are included with the rendering source. Runner was exported from its current spear-bearing authored source, with visual timing mapped to existing game combat timing. See [README](README.md) for lazy-loading budgets and browser checks. Dedicated motion coverage includes Healer healing, Dragon breath and Slime death. Paladin healing holds a neutral pose; other roles retain their final pose for the short corpse fade. Runner's authored 640/320 ms attack/contact is sampled at 4/3 speed to preserve the game's existing 480/240 ms timing.
